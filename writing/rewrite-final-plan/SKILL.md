# Rewrite Final Plan

Turn a revised plan or design into a standalone final version.

## Instructions

1. Read the current plan and enough of the preceding conversation to understand the intended final design.
2. Rewrite the whole plan using these instructions:
   - Rewrite the final design document in full as if it had been planned in that form from the beginning.
   - Assume its readers know nothing about the history or background of the conversation. Leave no text that only makes sense with that context.
3. Return the rewritten plan. Keep the target document's language and useful structure unless the user requests another format.
